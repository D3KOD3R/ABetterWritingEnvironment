The checked-in JSON files are compact lookup shards generated for A Better Novel Authoring Environment by `scripts/build-open-english-wordnet-index.mjs`. They retain definitions, examples, pronunciations where provided, synonyms from synset members, and WordNet exception-form aliases needed for offline manuscript lookup.
